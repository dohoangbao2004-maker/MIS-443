# 💡 Why This Course Matters

Business Analysts are expected to answer questions with data.

To do that, they must first understand how data is stored, managed, queried, and transformed.

MIS443 builds that foundation by introducing relational databases, SQL, PostgreSQL, and data management concepts that support data-driven business decision making.

Throughout this course, I am learning not only **how to write SQL**, but also **how to think systematically about business data**.
