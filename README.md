# 💡 Key Findings

### 📈 Revenue Trend

The store's revenue remained relatively stable over the analyzed period rather than showing a clear long-term growth or decline pattern.

### 🌎 Country Performance

The United States represents the strongest market in the dataset, combining high total revenue with strong customer spending.

### 🎵 Genre Performance

Rock has the largest catalog and generates the highest revenue, but catalog size does not consistently correspond to revenue performance across genres.

### 🚫 Unsold Catalog

**1,519 tracks, representing 43.4% of the catalog, have never generated a sale.**

The unsold content is concentrated in particular genres, showing that demand is uneven across the catalog.

### 👥 Sales Agents

Sales performance needs to be interpreted alongside customer assignment levels rather than using total revenue alone.

### 💰 Revenue Accumulation

Cumulative revenue analysis shows how the store progressively generated revenue over time and identifies the point at which cumulative revenue first exceeded **$1,000**.
